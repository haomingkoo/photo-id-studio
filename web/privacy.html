Who we are

Photo Compliance Studio (studio.kooexperience.com) is a personal project run by Haoming Koo in Singapore. It checks whether a photo meets passport or ID photo rules and returns a cropped version.

What we process

  • Your photo. A photo of a face is personal data. You upload it so we can run the compliance check.
  • Face measurements. Software on our own server finds face landmarks and separates the person from the background. We use these measurements only to check framing, lighting and background. We do not identify anyone or match faces against other photos.
  • Photo metadata. We read the orientation and capture date stored in the photo file. The capture date is shown back to you in the report. The images we return do not carry the original metadata.
  • The file name. It is used only to read the file extension and may appear in the report sent back to you.
  • Your IP address. Used for rate limiting (see below).

How your photo is handled

  • Your photo is held in server memory only while the check runs. The app never writes it to disk or to a database.
  • When the response is sent, the photo and every image made from it are discarded from the server. The results exist only in your browser until you close the tab.
  • Photos are not used to train any model. They are not shared with or sent to any AI or face-recognition service.
  • Nobody else can see your photo or results. They are returned only in the response to your own request.

Photos of other people and of children

Upload a photo only if it is your own, or if you have permission from the person in it. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must give that permission. You confirm this with the checkbox before each upload, and the service rejects uploads without it.
