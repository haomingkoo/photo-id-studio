Privacy

Updated 25 October 2026

Who runs this

Photo ID Studio is a free personal project by Koo Haoming in Singapore. It checks whether a photo meets passport or ID photo rules. Questions or requests go to privacy@kooexperience.com.

What happens to your photo

  • Your photo stays in server memory only while the check runs. It is never written to disk or a database.
  • Once the result is sent back, the photo and every image made from it are thrown away.
  • We find face landmarks to check framing and lighting. We do not identify anyone or compare faces.
  • Photos are never used for training. They are never sent to any AI or face recognition service.
  • Nobody else can see your photo or result.

Photos of other people

Only upload your own photo or one you have permission to use. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must agree. You confirm this with the checkbox before each upload.

Who else is involved

  • Railway hosts the server.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site over HTTPS. It also counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and never sees your photo.

Your choices

You can stop at any time by not uploading. Photos are not kept, so there is normally nothing to delete. For questions or complaints, email privacy@kooexperience.com. You can also contact Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

Report misuse

If someone uploaded your photo without permission, email privacy@kooexperience.com.
