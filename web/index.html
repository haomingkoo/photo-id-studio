How to take a photo that passes

A phone, a plain wall and soft daylight are enough. Follow these steps, then check the photo here.

Taking the photo

  1. Stand about half a metre in front of a plain white or off-white wall.
  2. Face a window or a soft lamp so light falls evenly on both sides of your face.
  3. Ask someone to hold the phone at your eye level, or prop it up and use the timer.
  4. Use the rear camera if you can. If you use the front camera, turn off mirroring.
  5. Look straight at the lens with your mouth closed and both eyes open.
  6. Keep your whole head, your hair and both shoulders in the frame, with space above your head.
  7. Upload the original photo file, not a screenshot or a photo sent through a chat app.

What we check

CheckWhat passes
SizeThe photo is large enough to crop to the document's exact pixel size.
One faceExactly one person, facing the camera.
PoseHead straight, chin level, eyes open, mouth closed.
FramingYour head fills the right share of the frame, with hair and shoulders showing.
Sharpness and lightYour face is in focus, evenly lit, and not too dark or too bright.
BackgroundPlain and bright, with no text or watermarks.
DateTaken recently, if the photo's date is saved in the file.

We only crop the photo and adjust its light and colour. We never redraw your face or background.

Automated checks can miss things. Always read the issuing office's own rules before you apply.
