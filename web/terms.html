The service

Photo Compliance Studio is a free tool run by Haoming Koo in Singapore. It checks a photo against passport and ID photo rules for a chosen country and returns a cropped version with error codes. The source code is public on GitHub.

Photos you upload

  • Upload only your own photo, or a photo you have permission to process from the person in it.
  • For anyone under 18, you need permission from their parent or guardian. If you are under 18 yourself, ask a parent or guardian to do the upload.
  • Do not upload unlawful content or photos taken without the subject's knowledge.
  • You keep all rights in your photo. You allow us to process it only to run the check you request. How we handle it is described in the Privacy Notice.
